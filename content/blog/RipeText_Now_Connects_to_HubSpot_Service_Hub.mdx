Your team closes hundreds of HubSpot tickets a week. Each one tells a small story - a frustrated customer, a broken workflow, a recurring question nobody has time to count. None of them set off alarms on their own. But put them together and patterns start to show.

The problem is that HubSpot's reporting gives you counts. Open tickets, closed tickets, average time to close. It tells you _how many_ but not _why_. Figuring out the "why" means exporting CSVs, reading through threads, and building pivot tables until you run out of afternoon. That works when you have fifty tickets a month. It falls apart at five hundred.

RipeText now connects directly to HubSpot Service Hub, and the setup takes about a minute.

## What the integration does

Authorize your HubSpot account through OAuth (one click, no API keys to copy) and RipeText pulls in your existing ticket history automatically. From that point on, every new ticket flows in as it happens.

But it doesn't stop at the ticket subject line. RipeText pulls the **full conversation thread**: the ticket itself, internal notes your agents leave, and email exchanges attached to it. It also resolves who's involved - matching customer contacts, companies, and the agent assigned to each ticket.

All of this feeds into the same AI analysis pipeline that already powers RipeText for Zendesk, Intercom, Front, and Crisp users.

## What you get

- **Topic clustering.** RipeText groups similar tickets automatically. Instead of reading through hundreds of threads to notice that "login issues" spiked this week, you see it on your dashboard the same day it starts.
- **Sentiment tracking.** Every conversation carries a tone. RipeText tracks that tone across your ticket volume so you can spot shifts before they show up in your CSAT scores.
- **Trend detection.** A new issue starts appearing in five tickets on Monday. By Wednesday it's in thirty. RipeText flags the trend early - before it becomes a fire drill.
- **One dashboard for everything.** If your team uses HubSpot alongside Zendesk or Intercom, you no longer need to check each tool separately. RipeText brings all your support channels into a single view.

## How it works

<BlogImage src="/images/blog/_uploads/846b1c8f-65fd-4876-b9a7-c0b27f4ec816.png" />

1. **Connect.** Go to Integrations, click HubSpot, and authorize your account. That's it.
2. **Import.** RipeText pulls your existing tickets - up to your full history - and runs them through analysis.
3. **Stay current.** New tickets, notes, and emails sync in real time. No cron jobs, no manual exports.
