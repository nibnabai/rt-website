The Integration API connects any support system to RipeText: an in-house helpdesk, a legacy ticketing tool, or a platform we don't integrate with yet. Unlike the other integrations, it's set up by a developer: RipeText defines the data format, and your code sends your conversations in that format.

## What it gives you

- Conversations in RipeText in real time, as they're created and updated
- History imported from a small service you host, called an **adapter**
- History uploaded in batches instead, if your systems can't accept inbound connections
- Several systems on the same RipeText account, each with its own credentials

## How it works

There are two directions of traffic. You need the first; the second is optional.

1. **You send to RipeText.** Whenever a conversation changes, your code sends it to [`POST /v1/support/events`](/docs/api/support/endpoints/send-event). This is how live updates arrive.
2. **RipeText calls your adapter.** If you run an [adapter](/docs/api/support/adapter), RipeText uses it to import your history, and to fetch a conversation when you send only its ID.

If your systems sit behind a firewall, skip the adapter: send full conversations, and upload your history with [`POST /v1/support/conversations/batch`](/docs/api/support/endpoints/upload-batch).
