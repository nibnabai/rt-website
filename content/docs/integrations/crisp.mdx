Connect Crisp to RipeText to analyze your live chat conversations and improve team performance.

## Overview

The Crisp integration lets you:

- Import conversation history from your Crisp website
- Receive real-time updates when new messages arrive via webhooks
- Analyze conversation topics, sentiment, and response quality

## Prerequisites

- Access to your Crisp workspace dashboard
- Ability to install plugins from the Crisp Marketplace

## Connecting Crisp

You can connect Crisp in two places:

### During Onboarding

When setting up your RipeText account, you'll be prompted to connect your support platform.

1. Click the **Add** button next to the Crisp integration.

![Click Add next to the Crisp integration](/images/docs/integrations/integration-crisp-onboarding-1.png)

2. Add your Website ID and click **Connect**. You'll be redirected to the Crisp Marketplace to install the RipeText plugin on your website.

![Install RipeText plugin from Crisp Marketplace](/images/docs/integrations/integration-crisp-onboarding-2.png)

3. Approve the plugin installation. You'll be redirected back to RipeText.

![Approve plugin installation](/images/docs/integrations/integration-crisp-onboarding-3.png)

After installing the plugin, RipeText will automatically kick off a historical import of your conversations so insights are ready from the start.

### From Settings

Go to **Settings → Integrations** in your RipeText dashboard.

1. Click **Connect** next to Crisp.

![Click Connect next to Crisp in Settings](/images/docs/integrations/integration-crisp-settings-1.png)

2. In the dialog that appears, add your Website ID and click **Connect & Analyze**.

![Connect & Analyze dialog for Crisp](/images/docs/integrations/integration-crisp-settings-2.png)

You'll be taken to the Crisp Marketplace to install the RipeText plugin. Once installed, your Crisp website ID is linked automatically. If this is the first time connecting Crisp, a historical import will run automatically.

## What Gets Imported

For each conversation, RipeText imports:

- **Messages** — the full message thread between operators and visitors
- **Metadata** — conversation state, segments/tags, and subject lines
- **People** — visitor profiles, associated companies, and assigned operators

## What Happens After Connecting

1. **Historical import** — RipeText imports your past conversations. Depending on volume, this may take a few minutes to several hours.
2. **Real-time sync** — New messages and conversations are synced automatically via webhooks going forward.

## Troubleshooting

### Plugin Installation Failed

If you can't install the RipeText plugin:

- Verify you have permission to install plugins in your Crisp workspace
- Ensure your Crisp plan supports third-party plugin integrations
- Try clearing your browser cache and attempting the installation again

### Missing Conversations

If some conversations aren't appearing:

- Allow up to 30 minutes for the initial import to complete
- Only conversations with at least one message are imported
- Check the integration status in **Settings → Integrations**

### Webhooks Not Firing

If new messages aren't syncing in real time:

- Verify the RipeText plugin is still active on your Crisp website
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting the integration from **Settings → Integrations**
