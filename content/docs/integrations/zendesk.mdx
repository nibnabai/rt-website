Connect Zendesk to RipeText to import tickets and analyze your support team's performance.

## Overview

The Zendesk integration lets you:

- Import ticket history from your Zendesk instance
- Receive real-time ticket updates via webhooks
- Analyze ticket topics, resolution times, and agent performance

## Prerequisites

- Admin access to your Zendesk instance

## Connecting Zendesk

You can connect Zendesk in two places:

### During Onboarding

When setting up your RipeText account, you'll be prompted to connect your support platform. Enter your Zendesk subdomain (e.g., `yourcompany` for `yourcompany.zendesk.com`), authorize access, and RipeText will automatically kick off a historical import of your tickets so insights are ready from the start.

### From Settings

Go to **Settings → Integrations** in your RipeText dashboard and click **Connect** next to Zendesk. Enter your subdomain, then authorize via the OAuth consent screen. If this is the first time connecting Zendesk, a historical import will run automatically.

## What Happens After Connecting

1. **Historical import** — RipeText imports your past tickets. Depending on volume, this may take a few minutes to several hours.
2. **Real-time sync** — New tickets and updates are synced automatically via webhooks going forward.

## Troubleshooting

### Authorization Failed

If the OAuth flow fails:

- Verify you have admin access to Zendesk
- Ensure your Zendesk plan includes API access
- Check that OAuth applications are enabled in Zendesk admin settings

### Incomplete Import

If some tickets are missing after import:

- Archived or deleted tickets may not be available via the API
- Allow up to 30 minutes for the initial import to complete
- Check the integration status in **Settings → Integrations**
