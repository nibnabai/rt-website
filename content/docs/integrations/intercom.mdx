Connect Intercom to RipeText to analyze your live chat and messaging conversations.

## Overview

The Intercom integration lets you:

- Import conversation history from Intercom
- Receive real-time updates when conversations are created or updated
- Track response times, quality, and topic trends

## Prerequisites

- Admin access to your Intercom workspace

## Connecting Intercom

You can connect Intercom in two places:

### During Onboarding

When setting up your RipeText account, you'll be prompted to connect your support platform. After authorizing Intercom, RipeText will automatically kick off a historical import of your conversations so insights are ready from the start.

### From Settings

Go to **Settings → Integrations** in your RipeText dashboard and click **Connect** next to Intercom. You'll be redirected to Intercom's OAuth consent screen — review the permissions and click **Authorize** to grant access. If this is the first time connecting Intercom, a historical import will run automatically.

## What Happens After Connecting

1. **Historical import** — RipeText imports your past conversations. Depending on volume, this may take a few minutes to several hours.
2. **Real-time sync** — New conversations and updates are synced automatically via webhooks going forward.

## Troubleshooting

### Authorization Failed

If the OAuth flow fails:

- Confirm you have admin privileges in Intercom
- Ensure your Intercom plan supports API access
- Try clearing your browser cache and cookies

### Delayed Sync

If conversations aren't syncing in real time:

- Webhook delivery can occasionally be delayed by Intercom
- Check the integration status in **Settings → Integrations**
- If delays persist, try disconnecting and reconnecting the integration
