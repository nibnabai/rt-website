Connect your Front account to RipeText to import conversations, extract insights, and monitor team performance.

## Overview

The Front integration lets you:

- Import historical conversations from your Front workspace
- Receive real-time updates via webhooks when new messages arrive
- Analyze conversation quality, topics, and sentiment

## Prerequisites

- Admin access to your Front workspace

## Connecting Front

You can connect Front in two places:

### During Onboarding

When setting up your RipeText account, you'll be prompted to connect your support platform.

1. Click the **Add** button next to the Front integration.

![Click Add next to the Front integration](/images/docs/integrations/integration-front-onboarding-1.jpeg)

2. Click **Connect** to begin the OAuth flow.

![Click Connect to authorize Front](/images/docs/integrations/integration-front-onboarding-2.jpeg)

3. You'll be redirected to Front's authorization page. Review the permissions and approve access.

![Front authorization page](/images/docs/integrations/integration-front-onboarding-3.jpeg)

After authorizing Front, RipeText will automatically kick off a historical import of your conversations so insights are ready from the start.

### From Settings

Go to **Settings → Integrations** in your RipeText dashboard.

1. Click **Connect** next to Front.

![Click Connect next to Front in Settings](/images/docs/integrations/integration-front-settings-1.jpeg)

2. In the dialog that appears, confirm Front is selected and click **Connect & Analyze**.

![Connect & Analyze dialog for Front](/images/docs/integrations/integration-front-settings-2.jpeg)

You'll be redirected to Front's OAuth consent screen — review the permissions and approve access to your workspace. If this is the first time connecting Front, a historical import will run automatically.

## What Happens After Connecting

1. **Historical import** — RipeText imports your past conversations. Depending on volume, this may take a few minutes to several hours.
2. **Real-time sync** — New conversations and messages are synced automatically via webhooks going forward.

## Troubleshooting

### Authorization Failed

If the OAuth flow fails, ensure:

- You have admin access to the Front workspace
- Third-party integrations are enabled in Front's settings
- Pop-ups are not blocked in your browser

### Missing Conversations

If some conversations aren't appearing:

- Allow up to 30 minutes for the initial import to complete
- Check the integration status in **Settings → Integrations**
