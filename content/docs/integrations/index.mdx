RipeText integrates with leading customer support platforms to ingest conversations, extract insights, and help your team improve.

## Supported Platforms

- [Crisp](/docs/integrations/crisp)
- [Integration API](/docs/api/support) (for any system not listed here)
- [Front](/docs/integrations/front)
- [GitHub](/docs/integrations/github)
- [HubSpot](/docs/integrations/hubspot)
- [Intercom](/docs/integrations/intercom)
- [Salesforce](/docs/integrations/salesforce)
- [Zendesk](/docs/integrations/zendesk)

## How Integrations Work

1. **Connect** — Authorize RipeText to access your support platform via OAuth
2. **Import** — RipeText pulls in your historical conversations
3. **Analyze** — Our AI extracts topics, sentiment, and quality signals
4. **Monitor** — New conversations are synced in real-time via webhooks

## When to Connect

You can connect an integration in two places:

- **During onboarding** — This is the recommended path. RipeText will automatically import your historical conversations so insights are ready from day one.
- **From Settings → Integrations** — You can connect additional platforms at any time. A historical import will also run when connecting a new platform for the first time.
